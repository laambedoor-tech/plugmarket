Frequently Asked Questions

How does delivery work?

Most orders are delivered instantly via Discord or email. After payment, you'll receive your account credentials or activation instructions right away.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including credit/debit cards, PayPal, and cryptocurrency. Payment options are displayed during checkout.

Do you offer refunds or replacements?

Yes! If you experience any issues with your purchase, contact us on Discord within 24 hours. We offer replacements for defective accounts and refunds in certain cases according to our policy.

Are these accounts legal and safe?

All our products are sourced responsibly and sold in compliance with applicable terms. We recommend reviewing each service's terms of use. Your data security is our priority.

How long do subscriptions last?

Each product listing shows available durations (1, 3, 6, or 12 months, and Lifetime for select products). The exact duration is guaranteed from the moment of delivery.

What if I need help after purchase?

Join our Discord server for 24/7 support. Our team is always ready to help with setup, troubleshooting, or any questions you might have.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Due to instant delivery, orders cannot be canceled once processed. However, if you need to modify your order, contact us immediately on Discord and we'll do our best to assist.
